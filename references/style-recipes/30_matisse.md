# 30_matisse · 1947 Matisse "Jazz" paper cut

> Source: Migration Test C (independent agent implementation of read-only skill, 2026-10-04). The scene code was integrated and transplanted to `scripts/engine/scenes/30_matisse.js`.

The file name during the experiment: `scenes/k4_matisse.js` (line 167). Each frame is about 25ms, and the frame difference is 9.3%.

**Composition**: Wall = "Polynesia" double blue chessboard (`#1d4fb8 / #4f95dc`, 240×236 paper-cut blocks per grid), window = "Icarus" (cobalt blue background, black shape, red heart, yellow star), ground = emerald green `#1f9a5a` with black skirting strips, the tabletop is black paper-cut, and the chair is yellow paper-cut.

**Technology**
1. **Scissor polyline `cut(pts)`**: Take a point every 14–24px along the outline, add ±2–2.5px seed jitter, and connect the points with straight lines. Shapes generated by yourself are always processed in this way.
2. **Seaweed (key)**: a cone-shaped stem, plus 2×lobes of** round-headed capsule fingers** alternating left and right. The finger angle is `±(1.05−0.55u)`, the length is `width·(1−0.45u)`, and the width is `width·(0.34−0.12u)`. All sub-paths `addPath` enter the same Path2D, filled with nonzero once, and the union is obtained, and the projection is only counted once.** Don't use "Modulate width along ridges"**: The first version did this, and what came out was caterpillars and Z-shaped lightning.
3. **RIG characters hand-cut edges**: RIG does not provide point columns, so the entire characters layer undergoes pixel displacement. The static displacement field is `fbm(0.028x, 0.028y, 2)·9`, with one set of dx and dy, and only processes bbox (330,470)-(1600,980), about 15ms.
4. **Paper projection**: shadow offset (5,6), blur 4, `rgba(10,20,40,.3)`, representing paper nailed to the wall.
5. **Gouache marks**: Cache 2600 long pen marks (60–300px, width 6–22, half and half black and white, α .04–.13, tilt −0.25rad), the entire frame `overlay`.

**motif animation**:
- Wrap the seaweed around the root like a piece of paper with `sin(2.8t+φ)·0.13`, and curl the edges with `scaleX 0.93–1.0` to breathe.
- Icarus: 7 irregular yellow stars, rotating and flashing (scale `.82+.22sin(7t+1.9k)`); black shape drifting up and down; red heartbeat (`1+.18·max(0,sin 9t)`).
- Two white birds flapping their wings (scaleY `.55+.45cos 13t`).
- A piece of pink paper flies and falls (scaleX=cos 6t).
- The heat is two small white paper cuts.

**characters**: "Romanian Shirt" by Matisse. White wide-sleeved shirt, a cobalt blue embroidered belt (small triangles of red and yellow intertwined) on the front, red/blue embroidered horizontal belts that rotate with the arms on the sleeves (the clip is in the upperArm), a long red skirt + a black belt, and 5 paper-cut rolls on her blond hair. The facial features are only made of paper-cut pieces: black almond eyes, red lip triangles, and pink cheek discs.
**Pit**: The trajectory of the first version of the falling paper crossed the girl's face and changed to the left wall. Place a piece of white paper cutout under the counter, otherwise the black words will not be visible against the cobalt blue.
