# 14_8bit · 1985 8-bit pixel game

## Pipeline
1. **The whole picture is painted on a 240×135 low-resolution canvas** (1 low pixel = 8 screen pixels, the actual measured pixel block of the original film is 8px), and finally `imageSmoothingEnabled=false` is enlarged 8 times at a time `drawImage`.
2. **Background pixel by pixel `fillRect`** (cache `8b_bg`): Cross dotted wall = 6px grid, 3×3 crosses with 3px interlacing; wooden floor strips y=106–113, black seams every 24px; bricks 16×6+1px seams, interlaced 8. A row of beige highlights (`#fbe2a6`) on the upper and left edges. The sky in the window uses 3 levels of blue (`#0856ef / #47c4f8 / #aae9fb`), and a band with a margin of ±0.05 is used to make a `(x+y)%2` checkerboard jitter - this is the only way to do gradients on the NES.
3. **characters＝RIG The path is reduced to low-resolution coloring → spriteize**: each part has a separate layer (far arm/body leg/hair/face/bangs/near arm; cat body/cat head), each layer:
   - `getImageData` → alpha < 110 directly transparent (anti-aliasing) → the rest quantized to the 14-color characters palette (nearest color);
   - Draw a 1px black border on the outer outline (blacken any opaque pixels in the 4 neighborhoods of the transparent pixels).
   **Layered strokes are the key**: When you only stroke the overall outer outline once, the hair/face/arms will become one piece; only by tracing each component individually can you have the inner outline of the NES sprite.
4. **All facial features are pixel by pixel** (eyes 1×2 black + eyebrows, cat eyes 2×2 black + 1 white highlight on the upper left, pink nose 1px, blush 2×1), use RIG anchor point `/8` to round and position.
5. **RIG.drawCup is not needed for mugs**: If the AA path is reduced to 6px wide, it will be blurry. Directly draw a 6×5 white cup + two red strips + 3px handle on the low resolution, and follow `G.cup`.
6. **HUD draws at full resolution**: `PressStart2P` 48px (1 pixel of font = 6 screen pixels, 64px is too wide and will make WORLD 1-1 hit the Apple icon), 8px black shadow; the cat head/heart/apple icon is a string bitmap, 8×8 per grid.

## jeans
RIG.girl is a long skirt. The 8-bit version uses two sections of `RIG.taper` (hip `G.hipSeat`→knee (1222,724)→ankle (1214,872), width 92/76/56) to draw blue pants. The far side leg is one step deeper `#0838a8` and 26px behind; white sneakers + a red stripe.

## Small loop (the film only stops for about 0.23s ≈ 11 visible frames, all made into step animation)
- Cat tail: 15fps four-position cycle `[-0.55, 0, 0.55, 0]` (tail parameter of RIG.cat), "several pictures alternating" of pixel animation.
- HUD heart flash: lt 0.16–0.23 (≈ 630 frames) changes every 2 frames: full heart becomes white, empty heart becomes full red.
- Potted plant shaking: Leaves in the same window are offset by `[0,1,0,-1]` by whole pixels.
- Question mark block pops out gold coins: lt 0.06–0.22 half sine up 9px and back down.
- The cloud outside the window moves 1px to the left in 2 frames; the pixel heat has three columns of 1×2 points, 30fps, moves up 1px in each frame, and loops 6px.

## Trampled on
- When the RIG cat's head reaches y≈535, it will press the window sill (the window reaches y=590). This is the position of the shared skeleton and has not been moved; the 8-bit black stroke makes the overlap more conspicuous, but the transition relies on standard composition and is retained.
- `spriteize` reads non-premultiplied RGBA, don't divide alpha.
- Performance: Hot frame 2.5–6ms, cold start 11ms.
