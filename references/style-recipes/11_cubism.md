# 11_cubism · 1912 Analytical Cubism (Picasso/Braque)

File: `scenes/11_cubism.js` (~280 lines). Performance: warm average 13ms, maximum 19ms, first frame (cache creation) ≈ 100ms; deterministic pass; lt 0.34→0.46 motion difference 6.9% pixels. The duration of the film is 0.47s (T10 fragment transition 0.24s).

## Pipeline
1. **Full cache of base plate `cb_bg` - "Transparent overlapping surface"**: Core function `plane(g, pts, col, alpha, r)`: Each surface has a linear gradient, the starting point is the 0th vertex, the end point is randomly chosen another vertex, three color scales = mix 25% off-white → primary color → mix 40% dark brown, alpha 0.18–0.7. Stacking method: 18 hand-arranged large surfaces (according to the original composition position) + 9 radial fans under the table (emitted from (1000,1090), alternating light and dark α0.3) + 70 random 3/4 polygons (avoiding the year area on the upper right). When multiple layers of translucent gradients are stacked, a space like "transparent, interpenetrating, each side is bright and one dark" appears in Analytical Cubism.
   - Texture surface: 4 pieces of stipple painting (9px diamond grid r1.6), 4 pieces of line (7px spacing diagonal lines), one page of horizontal line paper on the left edge.
   - Braque short stroke sand texture: 9000 small strokes of 3–8 × 1.5px, 55% off-white α0.28 / 45% dark brown α0.16 - the "brick texture" of the original film depends on it.
   - 46 thin straight lines (1.3px α0.55) = passage.
   - Imitation wood grain `wood()`: draw S/6 wavy lines along the long side, add Gaussian bulging `14·exp(-(u-k)²/0.01)` at the knot scar position, and make 4 circles of ellipses on the knot scar. For the tabletop and right side boards (Braque's comb-grained fake wood). Rattan chair back = 10px diamond hole points + two-way diagonal lines.
2. **Window pane sliding**: The window view is cached separately into a 560×540 large image (55 blue-gray slices + 3 oblique beams + Eiffel Tower + Seagull Arc). The four frames are clipped and then framed with different offsets. `dx = sin(9lt+1.7i)·18 + 10fps jitter ±3`, `dy = cos(7lt+i)·8` - the same window view is misplaced in the four frames = Multi-perspective combination; each grid also has a translucent triangle sliding horizontally with 260px/s.
3. **CAFE template word jumping and transposition**: `AlfaSlabOne-400` 104px is drawn into an off-screen small layer, 12fps stuck (`st=floor(lt·12)`), sequence table `CAFE/ACFE/CAEF/CFAE` rotation, each step per word y ±13, rotation ±0.11rad (hash taken); then `destination-out` cuts out horizontal and vertical fractures = Template characters (the fracture reveals the folded surface underneath, which is more realistic than the color strips on drawing paper).
4. **Newspaper NEWS**: cache 300×190 thumbnail (LibreBaskerville-700 84px "NEWS" + 12px title "THE BATTLE / A CAT JOINS IN" + three columns of gray fake text), each frame is slightly shaken at 8fps (rotation ±0.0125rad, x ±1.5px), upper right corner press `sin(20lt)` translated.
5. **characters = self-written slicer `facetize`**: RIG flat paint (fill mode, no outlines) into the off-screen layer → read back the bounding box pixels → jitter triangle network (randomly select diagonals for each grid) → each triangle picks the color at the center of mass, `×(0.8+0.38·rand)` light and dark, and then makes a light→dark gradient from vertex 0 to vertex 2 →** The triangle is not clipped** and drawn directly back (center of mass) skipped for alpha<120). Not cropping is the key: the edges of the silhouette are cut into jagged straight edges, and some triangles even extend beyond the outline - it gives the feeling of Cubism's "plane overflowing the outline".
   - Girl cell 58 (large surface), use cell 26 to cut the hair area finely, only cut the "color like blonde" (`only: R>150 && B<130 && R-B>60`), cat cell 26.
   - Vertex jitter ±8% cell every 8fps, 16-35% triangle light and dark jump ±12% (boil) → characters are "slightly moving", this is the case in the cat area in the original film at 548-562 frames.
6. **Picasso-like structure after cutting and patching**: Re-pave two skin color light and dark surfaces on the face (the cut surface will bring the blonde color into the face) + blue-gray surface of the front half of the face α0.82 + frontal almond eyes (frontal eyes are drawn on the side face) + spiral ears + red lips; two light and dark triangles on the white collar + three buttons; 8 radiating tangent lines on the skirt from the waist + alternating light and dark wedges; use straight segments for the outer contour `segs()`, each end of each segment extends 10–16px (the line is drawn out of bounds). Cat: hexagonal straight head outline, `bodyPts` straight body outline, yellow rhombus eyes + vertical pupils, side triangular nose.
7. The top five α0.2 stacked surfaces are pressed against the human and cat bodies and moved in `sin(6lt)·8` direction—the characters are “embedded” into the space, unlike being attached to the background.

## Step on the pit (important)
- **Uncertainty: The first frame is different from the next.**  Two reasons overlap, both only appear in the first frame: ① The off-screen layer of `getImageData` is drawn on the GPU for the first time. After reading it back, Chrome converts it into a CPU raster. The anti-aliasing is different → the center of mass color selection is different → the triangle color is different; ② More importantly: the context state of the off-screen layer (`lineJoin='round'` and segs set in facetize) `lineCap='round'`) will remain to the next frame, and the first frame uses the default miter/butt. Correction method: Build your own layer `getContext('2d',{willReadFrequently:true})`, **Use `g.reset()` (clear pixel + clear state) at the beginning of each frame instead of `clearRect`**. Use hash to compare "the same lt is rendered three times" to find out.
- Cell 34 is full of small triangles, like a low-poly 3D model rather than cubism; only cell 58 has "large light and dark surfaces".
- In the second cut of the hair area, I used a round clip at the beginning, and cut half of the face into gold color - added `only` filtered by color.
