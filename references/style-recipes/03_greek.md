# 03_greek · 530 BC Attic black painted pottery - code drawing notes

File: `scenes/03_greek.js` (pure Canvas 2D, no images). Final self-test: warm max 15.9ms / mean 7.6ms / cold 80ms, deterministic pass, 0.2↔0.7s motion difference 22% pixels are moving.

## One sentence trick
Black painting = **Black glaze silhouette + engraved line + additional color** three-piece set, each one is indispensable:
- Silhouette: Fill in `#1b120d` (near black with warmth) for all persons/objects, no outline, and rely on the contrast between the silhouette and the clay base to form the shape.
- Engraved lines: "Carve through" the black silhouette, using clay bright orange `#e8904c` to draw 1.5–2px thin lines (the boundaries between folds, facial features, and overlapping limbs).
- Additional colors: purple-red `#8a2a37` ribbon (headband, cuffs, hem, collar, cushion) + white dots (`#f6ead6`, r≈2, spacing 11–17px).
If you do these three things, it will look like a Greek pottery vase without any texture.

## Pipelines and parameters
1. **Clay bottom (cache `gr_bg`)**: 480×270 low-resolution pixel-by-pixel calculation fbm(×0.016, 4 octave) mottled ±30 + `noise(x*0.006, y*0.85)`’s **horizontal band noise** (blow pattern) ±9, enlarged to 1920×1080 (low frequency is inherently blurry, saving 16 times calculation); then draw 560 Random horizontal thin lines (length 150–1050, alpha 0.03–0.08, both dark/light) are used as fine lines; 420 dark dots + 120 light dots; two layers of `P.grain`; 5 cracks (every 9px section, ±2px jitter, and a 1px offset light edge on the lower edge, the cracks immediately have thickness). Center stack a layer of Warm Radial Lightening 0.16. Cold start full frame ~80ms.
2. **Vignette (cache `gr_vig`)**: Radial 380→1180, outer edge rgba(30,6,0,.62), the last layer covers the entire screen (including stripes).
3. **Static black painting layer (cache `gr_static`)**: picture frame (black border 18px + white dot string 11.6px spacing + inner thin line), grapevine (sine stem + triangular grape cluster 3-2-1 dots), table (legs with animal claw feet), turned chair legs (width table [y,w] polyline symmetrical shape), swan neck chair back (13px Thick stroked curve + elliptical head + triangular beak), amphorae (24-point symmetrical outline → `RIG.smooth`, clay + little black cat on the bottle panel, ray pattern on the bottom of the bottle, a vertical 0.09 gloss gradient), two English inscriptions in hand-painted lettering.
4. **English inscriptions**: `CHEERS AND DRINK` and `A LOVELY CAT` use a custom Latin stroke table (1–3 polylines per letter, unit cell 0..1), preserving the hand-painted pottery style. Character height 30px, pen width 3.4px, random height × (0.9–1.06), baseline ±6%, and vertex jitter ±0.7px. O is a small circle and I is 2px wide. Uneven heights give the inscription its handmade appearance.
5. **Scrolling pattern**: The top back pattern (period 60px, 7px black line, unit polyline `[[4,46],[4,0],[50,0],[50,34],[18,34],[18,14],[36,14],[36,22]]` + long bottom line) and the bottom bud chain + ray pattern (period 76px = two 38px units, odd numbered buds are filled with purple, so the real period is 76) are each pre-rendered into a wide W+2P strip, each frame `drawImage(strip, -(off mod P))`. Offset curve:
   - Top: `off = 22·lt + 60·easeInOut((lt−0.49)/0.35)`, left;
   - Bottom: `off = −(18·lt + 76·easeInOut((lt−0.477)/0.367))`, to the right.
   The original film only performs an easeInOut cycle jump at frames 169–191; I added an additional 18–22px/s constant speed drift so that the movement can be seen in the first 0.47s of this segment.
6. **Motif in the frame** (each frame is drawn, clip into the frame): 6 dolphins (self-written Bezier silhouette: beak, dorsal fin, pelvic fin, tail fork + abdominal engraved line + white eye spot) swimming back and forth ±9px along their own direction, angle ±0.12rad, period 0.85s, mutual phase difference 1.3; the ship as a whole moves around (560,420), undulating ±4px, shaking ±0.025rad, period 0.9s. White sails + purple stitching, crescent hull + scorpion tail, purple cabin, reclining human figure.
7. **Lyre**: `0.09·sin(2π·lt/0.8+0.5)` pendulum with the nail (1050,150) as the axis (±5°, displacement at the speaker ±17px, obvious within 1 second). Turtle shell speaker = black circle + engraved coil + 5 small engraved circles.
8. **Rose decoration**: 8 black ovals + carved lines + purple heart, `lt·1.6` rad/s rotating forward and backward alternately.
9. **Orange and white cat** (self-drawn, without using RIG’s default polygon; the tail is placed with `RIG.cat`’s tailPts and choreo.tail): the whole body is shrunk by (548,903) 0.9 to fit into the standard frame; the orange silhouette + black stroke is 3.4px; the white chest/white paws/muzzle are in "Clay White" cream color;** The tiger spots on the back are taken equidistantly along the back contour according to the arc length At 12 o'clock, draw a cone-shaped spot** (length 30–48, root width 10) toward the center of the body (575,800) - much more natural than hand-shaped spots; thigh arcs, vertical spots on front legs; antique-style big round eyes (white eyes + black pupils + highlights + an eyebrow arc above), change to arcs when blinking; purple-red collar with white dots.

- The top of the RIG cat's head reaches y=535, and the top of the cat's head in a standard frame is 610; if it is not compressed, it will be pressed to the bottom edge of the picture frame.
- When the odd and even buds of the bottom strip alternate, the real cycle is 2 units (76px). The wrap cycle of the pre-rendered strip must be 76, otherwise the color will jump every 38px.
- The seed of `P.grain` = key length. The keys of the two layers of grain must be of different lengths, otherwise the patterns will be the same.
- **The end point of the cup should be modernized (reviewed and reworked)**: RIG's cup→mouth end point is near the tip of the nose, and the cup is tilted −0.45rad. After changing to a wide and shallow Kiriks cup, it looks like a beak/pipe stuck in the face, and the hand covers the mouth and chin. Change to do IK by yourself: lip point rest(1243,391) is transformed to the screen using the head DOMMatrix, cup rim center = lip point −(30,−1) (the right end of the cup rim just touches the lip), inclination angle −0.08·cup (≤5°), hand IK target = cup rim +(lerp(32,6,cup), 30), that is, held at the foot of the cup; `RIG.ik2(S, Hand target, 165s, 122s, −1)`+`RIG.taper` own upper arm/forearm.
