# 16_2026 · 2026 Flat vector illustration + the ending of the whole film with a laugh

All performances are written in **Full film frame number `F = t*60`**, and key figures are copied line by line from `../breakdown/storyboard.md` §4, to facilitate frame-by-frame comparison with the original film.
Compare the contact table (original film on the left | reproduced on the right): `../renders/16_2026/cmp_E1…E4_*.jpg`, fragment `clip_2026.mp4` (10.8–15.06s).

## Painting style (Dribbble flat + grain)
- Solid color blocks, no strokes; body blocks are only divided by "color blocks one level darker in the same color series" (sweater shiny diagonal block `#f08b7d`, cat thighs `#f3ad4c` + a dark arc edge).
- **Granular stippling `stipple()`**: Sprinkle 2px square dots in the shape. The density is a function of position - pink circles `0.12 + 0.75·clamp((y-cy+120)/420)` (the farther down they are, the denser they are), the lavender blocks are denser toward the lower right, and the sun is denser toward the lower right. Static partial full-in cache `fl_bg`.
- After the characters layer is painted, use a static grain texture `source-atop` to overlay it (5% dark spots α70, 3.5% bright spots α90), and the particles only fall on the characters.
- Light cone: 26000 points, width linearly with y from 56→150, transparency attenuated with y, closer to the center, cached as a 340×420 texture, and oscillated around (1010,0) with the chandelier.

## Girl (self-written pose based on RIG)
- Torso/neck anchor point: `RIG.girl({s:1.12})`; adjust the head again to `RIG.girl({s:1.344, dy:43.7})` - equivalent to enlarging the head 1.2 times around the neck point, the large-head ratio of a flat illustration. The face, bangs, back hair, balls, eye/lip/ear anchors are all taken from it.
- Arm: `RIG.ik2(shoulder, target, 150, 140, -1)`, three sets of target click progress mixed: typing (both hands on the keyboard alternately rise and fall 7px at 22 rad/s) → touch the cat (688–704, draw a small circle on the cat’s back with the near hand) → exclaim (791–798, `ease.outBack` Lift it up to your chin, fingers spread).
- Expression: closed eye arc + raised mouth (<686) → dot eyes + highlights, small o mouth (from 686, o pops up with outBack) → big eyes + wide open mouth + tongue (from 791) → changed to winking "<" after 838 frames (the final frame of the original film also has eyes closed).
- Tension lines: 3 lines, radiating from the front and top of the head, length outBack pop-up; color 793 light pink → 796 full red (RGB interpolation); 830–888 random jitter ±2.5px every 2 frames, head synchronization jitter ±2px.
- Self-painted pant legs/socks/shoes (RIG is a long skirt).

## Cat (pose library + interpolation)
Each pose is a set of local key points (facing right, origin = center of mass): 10 points on the body, 6 points on the white chest, ellipse on the thigh, 3 segments on the back pattern, 4 Bezier points on the head, two front legs, hind legs, and tail, and gy (from the sole of the foot to the center of mass).
Postures: `SIT_F` (sitting on the ground, measured at 10.95 seconds from the original film), `SIT_T` (sitting on the table, the original film facing left after actual measurement and then mirrored to the right), `LEAP` (horizontally stretched), `LAND` (front paws down). `mixPose` value-by-value lerp.
Transformation: `translate(center of mass) → [around the sole of the foot or around the center of mass] rotate → scale(dir·sx, sy)`. Squeeze and stretch are all around the sole of the foot, so the feet do not leave the ground when squatting.
