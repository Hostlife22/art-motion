# Total style index (35 types, updated on 2026-10-05)

To choose a style, first look at this table and then open the corresponding card. "Quality" refers to the current implementation level (★★★ Recognizable at a glance and can be used directly; ★★ Recognizable but with obvious shortcomings; ★ Needs to be redone), not the difficulty of the style itself. The shortcomings are the areas that should be surpassed the next time you do this style.

## Mass distribution and next step

- ★★★ 20 species, ★★ 14 species, ★ 1 species.
- **The most common shortcoming is characters**: the background passes through the style renderer, just like that style, and the characters are painted flat with only vector skeletons as if they were pasted on (positive experience No. 8). The next round of improvement starts with "characters are also rendered through the same renderer/reshaped according to the style": Rembrandt, Picasso Blue, Ink, Ghibli, Dali, Marvel.
